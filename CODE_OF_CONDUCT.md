# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard: be respectful, constructive, and inclusive.

## Our expectations

- Be kind and professional in discussions and reviews.
- Assume good intent while giving clear technical feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior, never on people.
- Help make the project welcoming to contributors from different backgrounds.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language
- Trolling, intimidation, or bad-faith participation
- Sharing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block participation when behavior violates these expectations.
